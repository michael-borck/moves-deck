How to use it

Deal it. Work it. Commit to three.

1

Deal the sections

One printed deck per table of three. Sort into already-do / should-do / nobody-does. The piles are the honest map.

2

Catch it, on paper

Take one real AI draft per person and hunt its errors against the source, assistant closed. Then write the CHECK line that would have caught them.

3

Commit to three

Two or three tasks, the first prompt for each, one checkpoint you will not skip — and one habit you will stop.

Built for the week after the session

The deck runs as a working session: deal the sections, catch the errors in a real draft on paper, and leave with saved briefs, named owners and review dates. Card faces stay typographic on purpose — art lives on the box, the back and the wall.
